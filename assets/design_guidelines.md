# Design Guidelines

## Color Palette
- **Primary**: #FF2D55 (Safety Pink)
- **Secondary**: #5856D6 (Trust Blue)
- **Background**: #000000 (OLED Black)
- **Surface**: #1C1C1E (Dark Grey)

## Typography
- **Headings**: San Francisco (iOS) / Roboto (Android) - Bold
- **Body**: San Francisco / Roboto - Regular

## Components
- **SOS Button**: Circular, pulsed animation, always accessible.
- **Risk Badge**: 
  - Low: Green
  - Medium: Yellow
  - High: Red
